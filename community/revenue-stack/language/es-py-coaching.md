## Prefer

| Use | Avoid | Why |
|-----|-------|-----|
| **acompañamiento** | coaching | PY market uses acompañamiento for guided support |
| **proceso** / **metodología** | playbook (anglicism) | Professional services framing |
| **clientes** | usuarios | B2B coaching context |
| **automatizar lo repetitivo** | reemplazar el toque humano | Personal touch is the product |
| **asistente** | empleado de IA | "Empleado" triggers job-loss fear |

## M-tier ($500/mo) framing

- "Le quitamos el trabajo mecánico para que usted se enfoque en el acompañamiento"
- Not: "AI coach for your clients"

## Anti-patterns

- BetterUp / CoachHub US positioning verbatim
- Promising scale without mentioning human gates on client-facing content

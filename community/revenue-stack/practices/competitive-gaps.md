## Gap 1: Departmental structure vs agent soup

Hype products sell N generic helpers. DEMIURGE sells **named departments** (Hera, Apollo, Thoth) with souls, KPIs, and feedback loops. Message: "Not 12 chatbots — a finance dept and a sales dept that learn."

## Gap 2: Personal touch with human gates

Sintra/Artisan auto-send without review. DEMIURGE gates proposals, outreach, discounts. Message: "La IA prepara; usted aprueba lo que sale con su nombre."

## Gap 3: Production ops, not demos

YouTube swarms show one-shot tasks. DEMIURGE runs cron briefs, deploy monitoring, pipeline tracking. Message: "Ya está en producción con Rubicón — no es un piloto."

## Gap 4: LATAM-native motion

Global products are English-first, card-only, no WhatsApp. DEMIURGE: Spanish, WA-native discovery, local case studies, PY timezone.

## Gap 5: Memory that compounds

Credit-based SaaS resets monthly. Git-backed community memory + episodic memory = org gets smarter. Message: "El sistema recuerda cómo trabaja su estudio."

## Gap 6: Price vs value ladder

| Competitor | What you get |
|------------|--------------|
| Sintra $16/mo | 12 shallow helpers, 250 credits, stops working |
| VA $800/mo | Hours, not systems |
| DEMIURGE $240–500/mo | Production dept module with human oversight |

## Anti-positioning (do not claim)

- "Reemplazamos su equipo" — triggers fear
- "1000 agentes" without structure — sounds like hype
- "Sin intervención humana" — kills PY trust

## Deck slide 10 bullets

1. Swarm hype: no dept structure, no memory, no gates
2. Skeptics: right about hype; wrong that structured AI ops can't work
3. Real competition: VAs + freelancers — we beat on systems + Spanish + price
4. Moat: community memory + named agents + production proof
